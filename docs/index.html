Where each claim stands, and what would move it.

Every claim here has a public record: what was checked, under a rule written down first, and what is still open. Position on the board is read off that record. Nothing on this page is called proved or verified; a claim that meets its rule says so, and until then the board says what is missing and who could supply it. Anyone can take an open item; the work enters the record under their own name.
